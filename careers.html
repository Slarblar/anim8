Role Overview

We're hiring a Designer focused on brand identity and visual design systems for U.S. clients. You'll build end-to-end brand identities—from visual direction through to polished, handoff-ready systems—working closely with a fast-moving creative team.

What You'll Do

  • Build and develop brand identities: visual direction, key visuals, and brand guideline systems.
  • Design logos (concept → drafts → finalized) and all logo applications.
  • Work deeply with typography: hierarchy, grid systems, spacing, kerning, editorial layout.
  • Develop and maintain color systems (palette and brand tone) to ensure brand consistency.
  • Design communication assets: social posts, pitch decks, landing visuals, packaging (project-dependent).
  • Collaborate to translate brand systems into digital UI foundations (style guides, components).
  • Articulate design ideas and logic clearly, and execute quickly based on feedback.

What We're Looking For

  • Strong foundation in brand identity, typography, logo design, and color theory.
  • Modern, refined aesthetic with systematic design thinking and high attention to detail.
  • Proficient in Figma and/or Adobe tools (Illustrator / Photoshop / InDesign) or equivalent.
  • Receptive to feedback, proactive in learning, and driven to execute.
  • UI/UX is a plus (layout for web/app, design systems, basic UX thinking).

$700–$1,200 USD / month · + Bonus

Materials

Position Overview

We're seeking a 3D Modeler / Generalist with strong materials and texturing expertise to support our film and social media content production pipeline. This role requires someone who can seamlessly transition between modeling, texturing, and technical implementation while maintaining strict adherence to concept art. The ideal candidate excels at bringing 2D concepts to life through detailed 3D assets optimized for rendering.

3D Modeling & Asset Creation

  • Build high-quality 3D models from concept art, maintaining consistent aesthetics and style.
  • Model characters, props, environments, and vehicles across styles (realistic to stylized).
  • Develop efficient topology for animation-ready assets and real-time rendering.
  • Execute quickly according to artistic direction.
  • Create hero assets for close-up shots and optimize backgrounds.

Materials & Texturing (Primary Focus)

  • Primary responsibility: develop high-quality materials and textures using Substance Painter (+ Designer).
  • Create PBR materials that perform reliably across different lighting conditions.
  • Proficient in both hand-painted and photorealistic texturing methods.
  • Develop material libraries and texture standards for consistent look across projects.
  • Optimize texture resolution and compression for various delivery formats.

Technical Pipeline Integration

  • Prepare assets for export via FBX pipeline from Blender/Maya to Unreal Engine.
  • Set up materials and shaders in Unreal Engine for real-time performance.
  • Collaborate with the Lighting Artist to ensure material logic is correct.
  • Troubleshoot material and rendering issues across the pipeline.
  • Maintain organized asset libraries and naming conventions.

Concept Interpretation & Style Development

  • Work closely with art directors to accurately translate 2D concepts into 3D assets.
  • Adapt and iterate on artistic styles while maintaining quality and consistency.
  • Provide creative input on technical feasibility and artistic improvements.
  • Create style guides and reference materials for asset consistency across projects.

Generalist Support

  • Support various production needs including basic lighting, rendering, and scene assembly.
  • Assist with UV mapping, retopology, and asset optimization tasks.
  • Provide technical art support for pipeline improvements and workflow optimization.
  • Collaborate across departments to solve creative and technical challenges.

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years of professional 3D modeling and texturing experience.
  • Essential: Advanced proficiency in Substance Painter; strong skills in Blender, ZBrush, and Maya.
  • Essential: Experience with Unreal Engine material editor and real-time rendering.
  • Solid understanding of PBR (Physically Based Rendering) workflows.
  • Portfolio demonstrating versatility, materials expertise, and concept adherence.

VIDEO EDITOR /
VFX ARTIST

Based on Experience & Expertise

Editing &
Visual Effects

Position Overview

Anim-8 Studios is expanding its creative team and looking for Video Editors & VFX Artists to join upcoming projects. You'll work closely with the Creative Director, Producer, and Editor to create high-quality video content for global clients — including social media, brand content, and online entertainment. Depending on your strengths, you can focus on Video Editing or VFX, while collaborating with artists across the full pipeline.

Position 1 — Video Editor

  • Edit video content for social media, brand campaigns, and digital storytelling projects.
  • Cut footage, audio, graphics, and visual elements into compelling stories.
  • Work with creative briefs and collaborate with directors and producers to achieve the desired visual style.
  • Ensure smooth pacing, rhythm, and narrative flow in the final edit.
  • Handle the technical aspects of the editing workflow and project organization.

Requirements

  • Proficient in Adobe Premiere Pro.
  • Experience with After Effects and DaVinci Resolve at a basic level.
  • Ability to use rhythm, music, and visuals to convey messages and emotions through work.
  • Basic knowledge of Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator.
  • Ability to understand project briefs and communicate in English.

Position 2 — VFX Artist

  • Prepare and execute visual effects elements for video content.
  • Perform tracking, rotoscoping, cleanup, and keying.
  • Create simple 2D motion graphics and compositing elements.
  • Collaborate with editors and the creative team to seamlessly integrate VFX into video.

Requirements

  • Experience with After Effects.
  • Understanding of VFX preparation workflow (tracking, roto, cleanup, keying).
  • Basic 2D motion graphics skills.
  • Ability to work in teams and receive feedback positively.

General Requirements — Both Roles

  • 1+ years of experience in video editing, VFX, or a related field (MV/TVC experience is a plus).
  • Ability to understand creative briefs and communicate in English.
  • Good teamwork skills, proactive attitude, and positive work ethic.
  • Curious mindset — willing to research, experiment, and continuously improve.
  • Portfolio showcasing relevant work.
